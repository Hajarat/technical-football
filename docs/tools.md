The game stamps the commit it was built from: it prints `BUILD commit=... head=... stale=...` at start, warns on screen in interactive play when its build is behind the checkout, and records `build_commit`, `head_commit` and `build_stale` in the session log's `start` line.

The smoke run prints the round report on one line prefixed `REPORT ` and exits 0. `smoke.sh` checks it is identical to the batch tool's report.
Screenshots go to `game/out/` (ignored by git).

## Game options

Game options after `--`: `--data <dir>` (default `data/`), `--replay <file>`, `--quit-after-round`, `--quit-after-ticks N`,
`--shots <dir>` with `--shot-ticks a,b,c`, `--shot-on setup,live,charge,strike,flight,outcome,touch<N>` (the N-th touch) or `keeper` (a burst of frames whenever the keeper's action changes; pair with `--close-keeper`), and with a defender `defender` (a burst whenever his action changes), `hold`, `tackle` and `block`, or `--shot-burst <startTick>:<count>:<step>`, `--close-cam` / `--close-keeper` (close-up cameras for inspecting limbs), `--side-cam` (the close camera on the attacker until his first skill move starts, then fixed where it is, side-on to his run 1 m along it from where the move starts, from the side he does not move toward (the +Z side for a move without one), framing about 4 m of pitch: a planted foot stays on one spot of the frame while the body passes over it; pair with `--shot-burst`), `--frame-times`, `--perf` (per-frame engine and render cost, summary lines `PERF_*` at the end of a run: process and physics time, the physics tick split into the pad poll, the sim, the view update (with each figure's apply), the camera and the tools, CPU render time, draw calls, managed allocation and GC; the CSV has each section's time (`<section>_ms`) and managed bytes allocated (`<section>_bytes`) per frame, and `PERF_JIT_PREWARM` gives the methods compiled ahead at startup and how long that took; the GPU render time reads 0 on Metal; with `--perf-vsync on|off` (on: vsync and max_fps 60; off: uncapped, though a 120 Hz display still limits it), `--perf-csv <path>` for the per-frame rows, `--perf-warmup N` frames counted as warm-up (default 60), and the diagnostics `--perf-scale <0.25..2>` and `--perf-msaa 0|2|4` that change the GPU load), `--figure skinned|procedural` (default `skinned`: rigged human models; `procedural` keeps the primitive meshes; a missing or unloadable model falls back to procedural with a HUD warning), `--attacker-anim procedural|mocap` (default `mocap`: his running body from the stride cycles, needs the skinned figure, falls back to procedural with a warning when the cycles cannot load; `procedural` is the attacker's procedural run cycle), `--skill-anim clips|procedural` (default `clips`: a skill move with authored clips is drawn from them, in `--attacker-anim mocap` only; `procedural` draws every move as before, and it is what a move without clips gets), `--shot-anim mocap|procedural` (default `mocap`; `procedural`: the struck shot drawn by the figure; `mocap` draws it from the motion-captured kicks, in `--attacker-anim mocap` only, falling back to procedural with a warning when the clips cannot load), `--defender-anim mocap|procedural` (default `mocap`: the defender's stance, tackles, block, slide and get-up and the attacker's stumble and fall from the motion clips in `game/assets/anim/defender`, needs the skinned figure, falls back to procedural with a warning when the clips cannot load; `procedural`: the hand-built poses alone), `--defender-anim-log` (a `DEFENDER_MM` line at every switch of those clips), `--player-editor` (start with the player editor open, for screenshots: `game/tests/run-bg.sh -- --player-editor --shots out/editor --shot-ticks 40 --quit-after-ticks 45`), `--capture-panel` (start with the capture panel open, for screenshots, the same way), and `--background` (macOS: hide the app at startup and opt out of App Nap so a windowed run never takes focus; use it through `game/tests/run-bg.sh`, which launches it without activating it and prints the game's output; Godot may still flash in front for a moment while it starts).

Automated tools and agents must use `game/tests/run-bg.sh` for any windowed run (anything with `--shots`); headless runs need no wrapper.

The measurement lines the game prints in replay mode (`FOOT_*`, `STRIDE`, `SKILL_*`, `BODY_POSE` and the rest) are described in [animation.md](animation.md#measurements-printed-by-the-game).

## Probes

The keeper probes (`--keeper-probe`, `--probe-replays`, `--write-probe-replays`) and what the batch tool prints about the keeper are in [keeper.md](keeper.md#diagnostics).

`--skill-probe` prints, per move: gesture recognised, move made, possession kept 2 s after the move, the time from the move's end to 90% of the with-ball top speed with sprint held (`t90_s`), the same for the same turn without the move (`base_t90_s`, both medians), the ball's distance at the move's end, for a step-over or feint the ball's distance ahead of the player at the start, when its own motion begins and when the foot passes over it (`start_ahead_m`, `exec_ahead_m`, `mid_ahead_m`), and for a sole move the ball centre's distance from the foot's contact point when the sole lands (`sole_miss_m`), each as mean/max; then the move's length from its start, the sole's reach included (`dur_s`; a fake's from its contact), its speed steps (`steps`: ticks inside it whose ground speed changes faster than 15 m/s^2, all seeds) and those in the 0.3 s after its end (`exit_steps`, a speed-up no quicker than the speed law under the exit burst excepted), for the ball roll its largest running speed from 0.15 s after its start (`fwd_late_max`), the player's travel along and across the run, the ball's travel along and across it and the ball ahead of and to the side of the player at the end (`p_fwd_m`, `p_side_m`, `ball_fwd_m`, `ball_side_m`, `ball_end_ahead_m`, `ball_end_side_m`, means; across is positive toward the move's side), the largest forward ground speed from the first contact and the forward speed on the move's last tick (`fwd_max/release`), for a two-contact move the ball's largest distance from the move's path at the second contact (`c2_path_miss_max`), the ball's largest distance from the player from the first contact (`ball_dist_max`), the facing's turn toward the side (`turn_deg`, a spin's full circle) and the heel-to-heel's flick speed (`flick_mps`). It runs on at most 4 threads; `--seeds`, `--ratings` and `--only step_over,...` narrow it.

`--probe move-identity` prints one row per move and entry with what played it (`driver`: `legacy` or `sheet`), then a `CARD` line for each card with a missed band, a `PAIR` line for each pair of moves that look alike, and `IDENTITY pairs_failed= cards_failed=`. The ball measures are taken in the player's frame (centred on him, axes along his run at the gesture and across it; it does not turn with his facing) from the move's start to 0.3 s after its release: the furthest the ball gets toward the move's side and away from it (`side_to`, `side_away`; his right for a move without a side), ahead of him and back (`fwd`, `back`, negative behind him), each in units of 0.15 m, its lift above rest (0.05 m) and the move's own contacts (1). The body measures are taken over the move: his sideways range off his entry line (0.15 m), his total turn (90 degrees), his lowest pace as a share of his entry pace (0.15) and the move's length (0.08 s). A pair fails when fewer than two ball measures are a unit apart; two moves that never touch the ball are judged on the four body measures instead. Fake stop against fake cut is printed and not counted. A move has a card once its sheet drives it; the elastico's and the drag-back's are in the probe already and fail until then. The probe exits 0 whatever it finds. Measured with every move legacy: at a jog step-over and body feint (no measure apart), drag-back and fake stop, elastico and heel-to-heel, elastico and spin, spin and McGeady spin; from standing step-over and body feint.

## Session logs

Every launch except replay mode (unless it is given `--session-log`) creates `sessions/<yyyyMMdd-HHmmss>/` at the repo root (ignored by git):

- `session.jsonl`: one JSON object per line, flushed after each line, each with `t` (seconds since launch), `wall` (local time) and `type`:
  `start`, `pads` (with `vibration`, whether it is on), `input_mode`, `round_setup`, `round_begin`, `round_end` (with the full round report), `capture` (`id`, `round`, `tag`, `note`, world `tick`, `clock`, `partial`, `replay`), `round_abandoned` (a round that began and got no report: `round`, `reason`, `tick`, `clock`, `replay`), `selection` (`what`: `spot`, `attacker`, `keeper`, or `editor` when closing the player editor applied a changed player, with `abandoned` true if a live round was dropped), `warning` (data files, and the player editor's store), `pause`, `quit`, `controls` (a pad's face-button swap changed: `pad`, `swap_face_buttons`, `reason`; or vibration toggled: `vibration`, `reason`),
  `player_editor` (`action`: `open`, or `close` with `changed`, or `save`, `load` or `clear` with the `slot` (1 to 10), the `column` it was pressed in (`stats` or `presets`) and the `outcome`: `saved`, `overwritten`, `loaded`, `cleared`, `needs_confirm` or `empty`), `custom_sheet` (the full sheet, at each round start played with the editor's Custom player),
  `pass` (round, tick, `from`, `to` (−1 into space), `speed`), `control` (round, tick, team, `from`, `to`, `reason`: `pass` or `claim`) and `offside` (round, tick, player) in a round with a team-mate,
  and `skill` each time the attacker's figure starts drawing a skill move (round, world `tick`, `move`, `foot`, `side`, `stand_clip` and `run_clip`, `w_run`,
  `entry_speed`, and `drawing`: `clips` or `procedural`), so a play-test shows what was drawn.
- `round-<NNN>-<scenario>-<outcome>.json`: the replay of every finished round, saved automatically. Re-run one with
  `dotnet run -c Release --project src/FootballSim.Batch -- --replay sessions/<folder>/round-007-penalty-goal.json`.
  A round played with the player editor's Custom attacker has `attacker_id` `custom` and an `attacker_sheet` field holding the
  full sheet in the data/players format; the game's `--replay` and the batch tool's `--replay` use it instead of looking up the
  id, so the round replays without a player file. Replays without it run on `attacker_id` as before. (The probes that re-run
  replay folders still look up `attacker_id`.)
- `round-<NNN>-<scenario>-unfinished.json`: a round that began but got no report (the game quit or its window closed, or a round
  was set up over it, as when closing the player editor with a change), saved with `partial: true`: it holds every tick up to
  then and has no report.
- `capture-<id>-<scenario>-<tag>.json`: a capture's replay (see [controls.md](controls.md#round-capture)), with a `capture` block
  (`id`, `tag`, `note`, `tick`, `clock`, `wall`) and, when taken mid-round, `partial: true`. Two captures of one round are two files.

`sessions/captures.jsonl` sits beside the session folders and indexes every capture across sessions, one JSON line each: `id`,
`session`, `round`, `wall`, `scenario`, `attacker`, `keeper`, `tag`, `note`, `tick`, `clock`, `partial` and `file` (relative to
`sessions/`). Ids are `C<n>`, one more than the highest valid id in the file (C1 when it is missing), so they never repeat; a
line that does not parse is skipped with a warning. Notes are written as typed, so `grep "wrong foot" sessions/captures.jsonl`
finds them. Replays without `partial` or `capture` (every older one) read and run exactly as before.

The HUD shows the round number ("Round 7 · Penalty · ..."). A retry gets a new round number; its `round_setup` line has `retry_of` and the same seed.
Each finished round also prints a line such as `ROUND 7 GOAL penalty` to the console, a capture `CAPTURE C14 round 7 wrong foot`, and an unfinished round `ROUND 8 UNFINISHED penalty (<reason>)`.
